Case study

Walker & Wood Furniture Co.

One system for selling one-of-a-kind flipped furniture and running event rentals.

My family restores vintage, real-wood furniture. Some pieces sell; others rent out for weddings and showers. Off-the-shelf tools handled one or the other, never both, and nothing handled tagged pieces, deposits and signed hand-offs.

What I built

  • Rental quote builder. Customers pick pieces by tag number, set dates, and see delivery (by mileage), tax and a 30% deposit live.
  • Automatic paperwork. Each request becomes a Square customer, order and draft deposit invoice, plus an alert email, a confirmation and a Drive folder.
  • iPad ops app. A passcode-locked home-screen app: accept a quote (sends the invoice and adds calendar events), track deposits, and see sales waiting for hand-off.
  • Signed hand-offs. Rental check-out and check-in, and final-sale sign-off, with photos and a signature, produce a PDF that's emailed and filed automatically.
  • Brand + UI kit. Logo, wood-tone palette, Fraunces + Karla type, and the component set this page can wear.
